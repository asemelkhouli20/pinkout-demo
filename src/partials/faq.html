FAQ

Good questions, clear answers.

What exactly is PinkOut?

A women-first lifestyle, creativity and entertainment destination coming soon to Riyadh, with seven experience zones and an app in development.

When and where does it open?

In Riyadh. The exact date and venue will be announced once confirmed.

What are the fees or commission?

Commercial terms are shared after you apply, in the deck for your partner type.

Are any partners confirmed?

No partnerships have been announced yet.

Can we choose a zone or a themed day?

Yes. Pick them in the enquiry form, or open the form from any zone, package or theme and it's filled in for you.

How do we get the event partner deck?

Tick "Send me the deck" in the enquiry form. It's on by default.

Do I need a license to apply?

Yes: a valid license or professional certificate, plus a portfolio or Instagram that shows your work.

When do bookings start?

When the PinkOut app launches. Beauty services are booked through the app, at the client's home or at a PinkOut location.

Can I work at the event?

The event is run with event partners. Beauty service partners work through the PinkOut app: at clients' homes, at PinkOut locations, or both.

Can salons apply, or only freelancers?

Both. The form asks whether you're applying as a freelance artist or as a salon or beauty business.

When can I sell in the app?

When the PinkOut app launches. E-commerce partners sell through the app.

Can I sell at the event?

Not as an e-commerce partner: selling happens in the PinkOut app. If your brand wants a presence at the event, enquire as an event partner.

What can I sell?

Products that fit PinkOut's categories: beauty, fashion, accessories, sweets and treats, gifts and lifestyle.
