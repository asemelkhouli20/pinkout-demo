The miniature (3D model)

Same real floor plan (50 × 45 m), rebuilt as a detailed architect's model:

  • Soft shadows, warm light, wall thickness, floor finishes per zone (tile, wood, carpet)
  • Pink Passage: layered arches, mirror panels, floral garlands, light strips
  • Rose Hub: café counter + matcha machine, stools, round tables & chairs, fuller cherry-blossom tree, activation platform
  • Craft Studio: 5 stations: bead bar, phone-case table, laser engraver, embroidery machine, candy cart
  • Pink Market: little storefronts with striped awnings, counters, shelves with products, ice-cream cart
  • PinkOut Live: stage + LED screen, light truss, speakers, lounge seating (poufs, bean bags, low tables), moving beams
  • Move Studio: wood floor, mirror wall + barre, mats, plants, spa corner
  • Beauty Lab: foundation counter, fragrance bar, makeup & nail stations with ring mirrors, boutique shelves, mini-spa chairs
  • Visitors as little figures (body + head, varied outfits), walking the loop
PerformanceRepeated pieces drawn as instances; shadows only on desktop; the 3D runs only while on screen. Phones get the same model with lighter effects.
