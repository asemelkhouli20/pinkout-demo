Privacy notice for partner applications

Status: draft for legal review. Applications open soon. Until then, the application form on this site is a preview: it checks and reviews your answers in your browser, and nothing is sent or stored on a server.

What the form asks for

  • Your partner type and the answers for it (for example services, categories, zones, goals).
  • Contact details: name, email, phone (optional for event and e-commerce partners), organisation, job title.
  • Links you choose to share (portfolio, store or Instagram) and an optional message.

While the form is a preview

  • An unsent draft is kept in your browser's session storage so a closed form keeps your answers. It disappears when you close the tab. Consent is never saved.
  • Your language and motion choices are remembered in your browser.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts run on this site.

When applications open

Before sending is turned on, this notice will be updated with who receives applications, why, how long they are kept, and how to ask for a copy or deletion. Contact details will be added here.
