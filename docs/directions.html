Alexandria · عالمها الخاص

One family for Arabic and Latin: 700 headlines, 300 for the soft second line and body.

Idea
The PinkOut venue at night: the garden-portal facade, deep green and pink drapery, arches lit from inside. Premium, calm, confident; it tells sponsors "this is a serious, designed place."
Signature
The arch. The hero film plays inside an arch window with a brushed-metal outline; zone renders sit in arches; the 3D map is a garden diorama on deep green.
Motion
Slow and cinematic: outlines draw in, the arch "opens" (clip-path), and the camera glides. Nothing bounces.
Layout
Alternating deep-green and ivory bands; hairline rules instead of card boxes; generous space.
Avoid
Everything-pink; stickers and doodles; white text on pink; heavy drop shadows.
Best for
Sponsors and premium brands (your priority #1). Matches the official brand presentation.

IBM Plex Sans Arabic · عالمها الخاص

One family for both scripts: 700 headlines, 400–600 body. Already self-hosted on the consumer site.

Idea
The consumer site's joyful colour-block world, cleaned up for business readers: fewer stickers, bigger facts, one colour block per zone.
Signature
The colour-block zones. Each of the 7 zones owns a pastel; the 3D map is a candy-coloured diorama; one sticker per zone as an accent.
Motion
Snappy and springy: panels pop in, chips bounce once, the camera snaps to a zone.
Layout
Rounded 32px blocks on a sorbet canvas, a white invitation panel over the film, a pill nav.
Avoid
Sticker overload, rainbow chaos, childish copy; keeps the partner tone sharp.
Best for
Visual continuity with the consumer site; youth-focused brands (beauty, snacks, fashion).
