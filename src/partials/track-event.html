Know what your investment covers.

Before committing, agree the placements, activation space, timing, production responsibilities and content rights in your proposal. Also agree how you will assess the activity: for example, samples distributed, workshop participation or sales tracked by your team, where relevant. This gives you a clear basis for evaluating the partnership.
